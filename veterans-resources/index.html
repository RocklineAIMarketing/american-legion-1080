National Resources

What Legion Membership Connects You To

Beyond the post, The American Legion's national organization offers programs and support available to members and their families nationwide.

Benefits & Claims

Accredited Legion service officers provide free, expert help filing VA disability claims and navigating benefits.

Education

GI Bill guidance, state education assistance, and Legion scholarship programs for members and their families.

Employment

Resources connecting veterans with employers who understand and value military experience.

Health & Mental Health

Be The One — the Legion's mission to reduce veteran suicide through awareness, training, and peer support.

Financial Assistance

Temporary Financial Assistance for Legion families with minor children, and disaster relief through the National Emergency Fund.

Homeless Veteran Support

National advocacy and a dedicated task force focused on housing, health care, and employment for homeless veterans.
Be The One

Check In. Reach Out.

A simple phone call can make all the difference. As part of the Legion's Be The One mission, our members conduct Buddy Checks — reaching out to fellow veterans just to talk, listen, and make sure no one is forgotten.
