Who We Are

A post built on service, before and after the uniform.

American Legion Post 1080 has stood as a gathering place for Joliet-area veterans and their families — a place to find support, stay connected, and give back to the community that stood behind us. Every member here has worn the uniform; what we do now is look out for one another and for the veterans who come after us.

From honoring those we've lost to helping neighbors navigate VA benefits, from Friday nights at the bar to fundraisers that give back to local causes, Post 1080 is where camaraderie turns into action.
